What we do

Complete care for every smile

Prevention first, precise treatment when you need it, and follow-ups that keep your results lasting.

Checkups & Consultation

A thorough oral examination, with X-rays when needed, followed by a clear explanation of what we find. You get a written-out treatment plan with options, so there are no surprises.

  • Full mouth examination
  • Digital X-rays when required
  • Clear, step-by-step plan

Scaling & Polishing

Professional removal of plaque and tartar that brushing can't reach, followed by polishing to lift surface stains. A simple visit that protects your gums and keeps breath fresh.

  • Treats bleeding and swollen gums
  • Removes tartar and stains
  • Comfortable, no-rush visit

Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth with nearly invisible, removable Clearpath aligners. Your dentist plans each stage and checks your progress, so you smile confidently throughout.

  • Virtually invisible trays
  • Removable for eating and cleaning
  • Regular progress reviews

Tooth-Coloured Fillings

Cavities and chipped teeth are restored with natural-shade composite that blends in with your smile. We use a rubber dam to keep the tooth clean and dry, so the filling bonds well and lasts.

  • Front and back teeth
  • Shade-matched to your teeth
  • Rubber dam isolation

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Painful, infected or impacted wisdom teeth are assessed on X-ray and removed gently under local anaesthesia. You leave with clear aftercare instructions so healing stays smooth.

  • X-ray assessment first
  • Local anaesthesia for comfort
  • Written aftercare advice

Emergency & Kids’ Care

Severe pain, swelling, a broken tooth or a lost filling: we make room for urgent cases. Children are welcome too, with a gentle, friendly approach that builds trust early.

  • Urgent cases seen quickly
  • Gentle care for little ones
  • Call or WhatsApp us first

What are your clinic timings?
We're open Monday to Friday from 1:00 PM to 9:00 PM, and Saturday from 2:00 PM to 9:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays.
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead means no waiting. Fill in the form below or message us on WhatsApp to choose a time that suits you.
I'm in pain right now. Can you see me today?
Yes. We offer emergency dental care because relief shouldn't be delayed. Call or WhatsApp us on 0326 9779778 and we'll fit you in as soon as possible.
What happens during scaling and polishing? Does it hurt?
We gently remove hardened plaque (tartar) from above and below the gumline, then polish your teeth smooth. Most patients feel only mild vibration. If your gums are sensitive, we'll go slowly and keep you comfortable throughout.
Why do my gums bleed when I brush?
Bleeding gums are common, and usually a sign of plaque buildup and early gum inflammation. A professional cleaning and better brushing and flossing habits usually fix it. Don't ignore it, though: book a checkup so we can rule out anything more serious.
Do you offer clear aligners instead of braces?
Yes. We offer Clearpath transparent aligners, a nearly invisible and removable way to straighten your teeth. Book a consultation and we'll check whether aligners are right for you.
Do you treat children?
Absolutely. We see kids for checkups, cleanings and fillings, and teach healthy brushing and eating habits in a friendly, relaxed way.
